BEHIND THE ENGINE

How Radar works

01
Kraken market discovery

AssetPairs verifies each market directly against GBP. Ticker supplies snapshots and current bid/ask.

02
Five disclosed technical rules

9/21 EMA trend, close above EMA9, RSI 48–70, 20-candle breakout and volume confirmation.

03
Quality gates

Reject stale completed candles, wide spreads, insufficient GBP volume and unrealistic net risk/reward.

04
Honest results

4–5 passes may become SETUP, 3 become WATCH, otherwise NO SETUP. These labels are not probabilities.

Kraken pairs documentation Kraken OHLC documentation
Data boundaries: Kraken's public OHLC API provides up to 720 bars at a chosen timeframe, and the last candle is not complete. A Kraken app GBP display does not on its own establish a direct GBP trading pair. The scanner cannot discover coins before Kraken lists a GBP spot pair; the meme filter is an editorial watchlist, not an official classification. This is not investment advice.
